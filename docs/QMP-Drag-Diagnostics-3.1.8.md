## REL movement probe

position the source, then select and verify the unique queried relative pointer.
After the same two-second boundary and fresh routing check, send one LEFT down,
ten small held REL requests, and one LEFT up. Each changing direction axis uses

and down trial totals (-20,+20) counts, not the old (-962,+53) endpoint delta.
These counts are not calibrated screen pixels and do not promise a destination.

REL is a separate typed movement probe, not ordinary route execution. It retains
the original cursor and physical history, retires the live permit immediately
after successful transport completion and acquires one further diagnostic frame
under the exact retained owner/context/STOP checks. The normal result identifies
the evidence packet and any secondary persistence error; it is not a failure or
proof that a card moved. A subsequent experiment needs fresh planning/authority.
A post-probe capture failure leaves authority retired and retains the delivered
probe fact in the original failure details.
